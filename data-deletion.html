Delete My Data

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Local Data

If you never created an account, your progress is stored in your browser. You can delete it by clearing site data for Still Strong in your browser settings.

Cloud Account Data

If you created an account, you may request deletion of your account progress, pain logs, meal/habit data, custom exercises, notes, and progress photos.

How To Request Deletion

Replace this section with your support email before public launch. Example: email support@example.com from the email address used for your account with the subject "Delete my Still Strong data".

What Will Be Deleted

Deletion should remove the user progress database row and any files in the private progress photo folder for that user.

Future Self-Service

The production app should include an in-app Delete Account button after server-side deletion support is implemented.
