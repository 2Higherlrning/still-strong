Still Strong

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Overview

Still Strong helps users track joint-aware workouts, pain ratings, meal support, habits, notes, and progress photos. This information may be sensitive. We aim to collect only what is needed to make the app useful.

Information We Store

The app may store account email, workout progress, pain ratings, meal/habit tracking, custom exercises, notes, and progress photo metadata. If you add photos while signed in, photos may be stored in private Supabase Storage.

Local Storage

If you are not signed in, progress is stored locally in your browser on that device. Clearing browser data may remove it.

Cloud Sync

If you create an account, progress can sync through Supabase so it can follow you across devices. User data is protected by database security rules intended to limit access to the signed-in user who owns it.

Photos

Progress photos are private and should be treated as sensitive. Do not upload photos you are uncomfortable storing online. Signed-in photo uploads are stored in a private bucket and accessed through temporary signed URLs.

What We Do Not Do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your photos for advertising. We do not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Third-Party Services

The app may use GitHub Pages for hosting and Supabase for authentication, database, and storage. Their services may process technical information needed to operate the app.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account data and progress photos. See the Delete My Data page.
