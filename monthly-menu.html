Still Strong Meals

Monthly Family Menu

One dinner. Two plate styles. No separate cooking.

The goal is simple: cook one normal family meal, then make your plate more heart-friendly by leaning into vegetables, lean protein, beans, whole grains, olive oil, and lighter sauces.

Family plate

Keep familiar flavors, sauces on the side, tortillas/buns/pasta/rice available, and one fun topping.

Your heart-healthier plate

Half vegetables, palm-size protein, smaller starch, less cheese/cream/salt, extra salsa, herbs, citrus, or vinegar.

Week 1

Mon: Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas

Family: tortillas, chicken, peppers, onions, cheese, sour cream.

You: fajita bowl over lettuce or brown rice, extra peppers, avocado, salsa, light cheese.

Tue: Turkey Spaghetti Night

Family: spaghetti with lean turkey marinara and garlic bread.

You: turkey marinara over zucchini noodles or half whole-grain pasta, big side salad.

Wed: Beef and Veggie Taco Bowls

Family: taco meat, rice, beans, corn, cheese, chips.

You: lean beef or turkey, beans, lettuce, tomato, salsa, smaller rice, skip chips.

Thu: Salmon or Chicken Teriyaki

Family: teriyaki protein, rice, broccoli.

You: sauce on the side, double broccoli, smaller rice, sesame/green onion for flavor.

Fri: Turkey Burger Bar

Family: buns, cheese, oven fries, pickles.

You: lettuce-wrapped or open-face burger, roasted veggies, mustard, tomato, onion.

Sat: Slow Cooker Chili

Family: chili with cornbread, cheese, crackers.

You: bean-heavy chili, Greek yogurt topping, side salad, smaller cornbread.

Sun: Rotisserie Chicken Dinner

Family: chicken, mashed potatoes, green beans.

You: skin removed, extra green beans, small potatoes, olive-oil vinaigrette salad.

Week 2

Mon: Chicken Burrito Bowls

Family: rice, chicken, beans, corn, cheese, ranch or queso.

You: lettuce base, beans, chicken, pico, avocado, smaller rice, skip queso.

Tue: Pork Tenderloin and Roasted Veggies

Family: pork, roasted potatoes, carrots, applesauce.

You: lean pork slices, double carrots/greens, small potatoes, unsweetened applesauce.

Wed: Breakfast for Dinner

Family: eggs, turkey bacon, toast, fruit.

You: veggie omelet, fruit, one whole-grain toast, skip or limit bacon.

Thu: Shrimp or Chicken Stir-Fry

Family: protein, mixed veggies, rice or noodles.

You: extra vegetables, low-sodium sauce, smaller rice/noodles, peanuts or sesame lightly.

Fri: Pizza Flatbreads

Family: naan or flatbread, sauce, mozzarella, pepperoni, veggies.

You: whole-grain pita, veggie-heavy toppings, chicken, lighter cheese, salad.

Sat: BBQ Chicken Plates

Family: BBQ chicken, mac and cheese, slaw.

You: sauce on side, vinegar slaw, grilled veggies, small mac portion.

Sun: Lentil and Turkey Soup

Family: soup with bread or grilled cheese.

You: big soup bowl, extra greens stirred in, one slice whole-grain bread.

Week 3

Mon: Greek Chicken Pitas

Family: pitas, chicken, cucumber, tomato, feta, tzatziki.

You: salad bowl or half pita, extra cucumber/tomato, light feta, tzatziki on side.

Tue: Meatloaf Muffins

Family: lean meatloaf muffins, potatoes, peas.

You: turkey meatloaf, extra peas/side salad, small potatoes, low-sugar glaze.

Wed: Chicken Alfredo-ish Pasta

Family: chicken pasta with lighter jar sauce, broccoli.

You: half pasta, double broccoli, grilled chicken, use sauce lightly.

Thu: Fish Tacos

Family: fish sticks or baked fish, tortillas, slaw, sauce.

You: baked fish, cabbage slaw, corn tortillas, avocado, lime, sauce on side.

Fri: Chicken Sausage Skillet

Family: chicken sausage, peppers, onions, potatoes.

You: choose lower-sodium sausage, extra peppers/onions, smaller potatoes.

Sat: Build-Your-Own Rice Bowls

Family: rice, chicken, beans, cheese, sauces.

You: greens plus beans and protein, salsa, avocado, smaller rice.

Sun: Roast Turkey Tenderloin

Family: turkey, stuffing or rice, vegetables.

You: turkey, roasted vegetables, small starch, gravy on side.

Week 4

Mon: Turkey Sloppy Joes

Family: buns, turkey sloppy joe, baked fries, pickles.

You: open-face or bowl version, extra slaw, low-sugar sauce, smaller fries.

Tue: Chicken Parmesan Bake

Family: baked chicken, marinara, mozzarella, pasta.

You: chicken with marinara, light cheese, vegetables, half pasta or no pasta.

Wed: Veggie and Bean Quesadillas

Family: tortillas, beans, cheese, peppers, salsa.

You: one tortilla, more beans/peppers, light cheese, side salad.

Thu: Lemon Herb Chicken

Family: chicken thighs or breasts, rice pilaf, green beans.

You: skinless chicken, extra green beans, small rice, lemon/garlic instead of extra salt.

Fri: Taco Salad Night

Family: taco salad with chips, cheese, ranch/salsa.

You: greens, beans, lean meat, salsa, avocado, a few crushed chips for crunch.

Sat: Pasta Primavera with Chicken

Family: pasta, chicken, vegetables, parmesan.

You: double vegetables, chicken, smaller whole-grain pasta, olive oil and lemon.

Sun: Leftover Remix Night

Family: quesadillas, rice bowls, wraps, or baked potatoes.

You: salad bowl, soup bowl, or veggie plate with leftover protein.

Weekly shopping rhythm

  • Proteins: chicken, turkey, fish, lean beef, pork tenderloin, eggs, beans, lentils.
  • Vegetables: salad kits, peppers, onions, broccoli, green beans, carrots, cabbage, frozen mixed vegetables.
  • Family-friendly starches: tortillas, rice, potatoes, pasta, buns, flatbread.
  • Heart-healthier swaps: whole-grain options, low-sodium beans, olive oil, salsa, Greek yogurt, avocado, citrus.
  • Flavor helpers: garlic, onion powder, salt-free seasoning, vinegar, lemon/lime, herbs, pepper, smoked paprika.

Easy rules so nobody feels punished

Keep sauces and cheese on the side. Let the family use buns, tortillas, pasta, or rice. Build your plate with more vegetables and protein first, then add the starch you actually want.

This menu follows general heart-healthy patterns from the American Heart Association and CDC: more vegetables, fruits, whole grains, lean proteins, beans/lentils, unsaturated fats, and less sodium, added sugar, highly processed foods, and saturated fat.
