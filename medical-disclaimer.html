Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: May 30, 2026

Not Medical Advice

Still Strong is for education, planning, and personal tracking only. It does not diagnose, treat, prevent, or cure any disease or condition.

Talk With A Professional

Consult a physician, physical therapist, registered dietitian, or other qualified professional before starting or changing an exercise or nutrition plan, especially if you have osteoarthritis, chronic pain, limited mobility, injury, surgery history, heart conditions, diabetes, or other health concerns.

Stop Signs

Stop activity and seek appropriate help if you experience sharp pain, chest pain, trouble breathing, dizziness, fainting, sudden weakness, new numbness, unusual swelling, severe headache, or pain that worsens or does not settle.

Pain Tracking

Pain ratings and exercise swaps are self-tracking tools. They are not a diagnosis and should not replace professional guidance.

Emergency

If you may be experiencing a medical emergency, call emergency services immediately.
